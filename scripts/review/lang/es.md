## The language: Spanish

- **Neutral Latin American Spanish, the user addressed as tú** — never usted, never vos. Avoid
  Spain-only words: computadora (not ordenador), archivo (not fichero), celular/teléfono (not
  móvil), and no vosotros forms.
- **The family's rules (the user, 2026-10-17):** a plot beat stays **"beat"** (plural **"beats"**);
  a strand of the plot board is **"hilo"** (plural "hilos"); in a plural form the **number comes
  before the noun** ("3 notas", "{n} escenas" — never "Notas {n}" or "Su capítulo {n}").
- Sentence case for titles, labels and buttons ("Configuración del modelo", not "Configuración
  Del Modelo"). Days, months and language names are lowercase in Spanish.
- Questions and exclamations open with ¿ and ¡ — only when the English is itself a question or
  exclamation. End punctuation follows the English: a period only where the English has one.
- "beat" is the one English word that stays (the family's rule); an `untranslated` flag on a
  string that is only "beat" is answered in the reason.
- Examples of the error categories in Spanish: "beat" rendered as musical *compás*, "stale" as
  *rancio* (accuracy — the wrong sense); "reviravones" (fluency — not a word); usted, or Spain-only
  vocabulary (locale).
